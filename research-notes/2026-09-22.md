### Accelerating Dense LLMs via L0-regularized Mixture-of-Experts

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-09-22
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21672
- Matched layers: market_context

Existing acceleration methods often lead to noticeable performance degradation, while Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models require extensive computational resources. In this paper, we propose L0-MoE, a lightweight MoE approach using L0-regularization to accelerate dense LLMs nearly without performance loss.
