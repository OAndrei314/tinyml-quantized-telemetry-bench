### A False Average: Pooled CoT-Monitor Accuracy Conceals a Reasoning-Dependent Fragility

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-09-14
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00583
- Matched layers: market_context

On Terminal Wrench, about 77% of hacks are given away by the actions alone, and the monitor's pooled accuracy is dominated by them; on the remaining 23%, where the reasoning is the only signal, the same monitor is fragile. Monitor accuracy should be reported on the reasoning-dependent subset, where the defense is supportive.

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-09-14
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13427
- Matched layers: ai_compute
