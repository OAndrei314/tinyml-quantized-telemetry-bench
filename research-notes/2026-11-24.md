### A Cascaded Framework with a Cross-Attention-Guided Mamba-CNN Model for Pre- and Post-Treatment Glioma Segmentation

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-24
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29792
- Matched layers: ai_compute

This study proposes a Cross-Attention-Guided Mamba-CNN (CMC-Seg) model within a cascaded framework for pre- and post-treatment diffuse glioma segmentation, aiming to balance accuracy and computational cost. After preprocessing, a coarse segmentation model extracted tumor-centered patches, which were fed to CMC-Seg for fine-grained segmentation.

### A statistical measure of trust in generative AI

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-11-24
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30286
- Matched layers: market_context

The frequent absence of ground-truth knowledge or labels complicates verification of generative AI outputs. We estimate per-token trustworthiness of a student generative model as the statistical typicality of each generated token relative to an expert model's distribution, where the expert is used as a distributional reference rather than as a source of factual verification labels.
