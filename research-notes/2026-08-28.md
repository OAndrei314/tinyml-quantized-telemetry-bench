### A 12-CNOT Double Qubit Excitation Gate

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-08-28
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11733
- Matched layers: market_context

In this work, we presented, to the best of our knowledge, the first reported 12-CNOT decomposition of the double qubit excitation operator. As the double qubit excitation operator can be used as a building block hundreds or thousands of times in practical quantum algorithms, any reduction in such primitives compounds over the full circuit, resulting in significant overall resource savings.
