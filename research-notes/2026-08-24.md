### $Z^2$-ACT: End-to-End Verifiable Agentic Intent Control for Open 6G RAN

- Source: arXiv CS.AI

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21049

In order to host multi-vendors, it is essential that AI-assisted control loops remain safe, verifiable, and auditable under concurrent operator intents and untrusted model inputs. ### AgRefactor: Self-Evolving Agentic Workflow for HLS Compatibility and Performance

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-08-24
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30949
- Matched layers: market_context

We introduce AgRefactor, an LLM-based multi-agent workflow for refactoring software into HLS-compatible programs. Further agentic performance optimization yields a 6.51x geometric mean speedup over the SoTA pragma tuning tool and a 1.20x speedup over optimized open-source designs with less than 20% extra resources.
